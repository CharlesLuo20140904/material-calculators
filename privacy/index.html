Plain-language policy

Privacy

Project measurements are calculated locally in your browser. This version of the site has no accounts, analytics, advertising, or measurement database.

Calculator data

Measurements and prices entered into the calculators are used only by the JavaScript running on your device. Project Matter does not submit, save, or transmit those values.

Storage and cookies

The site does not set first-party cookies or use local storage. Reloading a calculator restores its default example values.

Third-party requests

The visual design loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives standard web-request information such as your IP address and browser headers. Source links in the calculation notes open external websites with their own privacy policies.

Future changes

If analytics or advertising is added later, this page must be updated before those services are enabled. Last updated October 16, 2026.
