Subgrade variation, forms, spillage, and product yield affect the final order.

Volume first. Then choose how to buy it.

1. Find slab volume

Length × width × finished depth gives concrete volume. Depth is converted from inches or centimeters before multiplication.

2. Add an allowance

The selected percentage covers reasonable variation. It is applied before the result is divided by product yield.

3. Round to full bags

Each comparison uses the published approximate yield and rounds upward because partial bags are not a purchasing unit.
