Drywall worked example

Fifteen sheets by area. Then check the wall plan.

A 12 × 10 foot room with 8-foot walls, one standard door, two standard windows, the ceiling included, 10% allowance, and 4 × 8 sheets produces a 15-sheet planning estimate.

Example result427 ft² net · 469.7 ft² required · 15 sheets

Reviewed by Project Matter · October 30, 2026

Calculate four walls and subtract large openings

The room perimeter is 2 × (12 + 10) = 44 feet. Multiply by the 8-foot wall height for 352 ft² of gross wall area. The calculator deducts one standard 21 ft² door and two standard 12 ft² windows, leaving 307 ft².

Net walls352 − 21 − 12 − 12 = 307 ft²

Measure unusually large openings directly rather than relying on the defaults.

USG guidance recommends subtracting large openings such as doorways and picture windows while ignoring small penetrations such as electrical boxes and pipes in an area estimate.

Add the ceiling as a separate surface

The 12 × 10 foot ceiling adds 120 ft². Net walls plus ceiling equal 427 ft². If the ceiling is not part of the project, remove it rather than trying to offset it with a lower waste percentage.

Apply allowance and round to sheets

At 10% allowance, 427 × 1.10 = 469.7 ft². One 4 × 8 panel covers 32 ft². Dividing 469.7 by 32 gives 14.68, which rounds upward to 15 sheets. Those sheets supply 480 ft².

The supplied quantity is 53 ft² above the net surface, or about 12.4%. Whole-sheet rounding explains why the final overage exceeds the selected 10% allowance.

Why the final order may differ

An area estimate assumes offcuts can be reused efficiently. A wall-by-wall layout may reveal additional sheets because seams must land on appropriate support, end joints are offset, openings interrupt otherwise reusable pieces, or the selected panel length cannot span the intended run.

  • Sketch panel orientation and seams for each wall and the ceiling.
  • Confirm framing spacing and supported edges.
  • Use the panel type, thickness, and fastener schedule required by the assembly.
  • Plan delivery, indoor storage, lifting, and ceiling support.
  • Estimate joint compound, tape, fasteners, corner bead, and accessories separately.
Fifteen sheets is a purchasing baseline, not a fastening plan.
